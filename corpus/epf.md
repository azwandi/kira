# EPF (Employees Provident Fund) — Employer Mandatory Contribution

Source: KWSP — Employer Mandatory Contribution
— from KWSP (Kumpulan Wang Simpanan Pekerja / Employees Provident Fund, EPF), "Employer Mandatory Contribution": https://www.kwsp.gov.my/en/employer/responsibilities/mandatory-contribution

## Employer Responsibilities

As an employer, your responsibilities include paying EPF contributions in respect of any person you have engaged to work under a Contract of Service or Apprenticeship.

You must ensure that accurate monthly contributions are deducted from your employees' salary and remitted to EPF.

Under Section 43(1) of the EPF Act 1991, and subject to the provisions of Section 52, every employee and every employer of a person who is an employee within the meaning of the Act is liable to pay monthly contributions on the amount of wages at the rate set out in the Third Schedule.

## When and What to Contribute

The employer must make the monthly contribution payment on or before the 15th of the month.

For example, a salary paid for January 2024 falls under the contribution month of February 2024, and it must be paid on or before 15 February 2024.

The employer must pay both the employee's share and the employer's share to EPF. Employers may deduct the employee's share from the employee's salary.

## Contribution Rates

The following rates are effective for October 2025 salary/wage (the November 2025 contribution month).

Malaysian employee below 60 years old earning a monthly wage of RM5,000 or below: employee contributes 11%, employer contributes 13% (Third Schedule, Part A).

Malaysian, below 60, wage above RM5,000: employee contributes 11%, employer contributes 12% (Third Schedule, Part A).

Malaysian employee aged 60 and above, at any wage level: employee contributes 0%, employer contributes 4% (Third Schedule, Part E).

Permanent Resident or non-Malaysian registered before 1 August 1998, below 60, wage RM5,000 or below: employee contributes 11%, employer contributes 13% (Third Schedule, Part A).

Permanent Resident or non-Malaysian registered before 1 August 1998, below 60, wage above RM5,000: employee contributes 11%, employer contributes 12% (Third Schedule, Part A).

Permanent Resident or non-Malaysian registered before 1 August 1998, aged 60 and above, wage RM5,000 or below: employee contributes 5.5%, employer contributes 6.5% (Third Schedule, Part C).

Permanent Resident or non-Malaysian registered before 1 August 1998, aged 60 and above, wage above RM5,000: employee contributes 5.5%, employer contributes 6% (Third Schedule, Part C).

Non-Malaysian who registered as an EPF member from 1 August 1998 onwards, any age and any wage: employee contributes 2%, employer contributes 2% (Third Schedule, Part F).

## Notes on the Contribution Rates

The minimum age to register and contribute as an EPF member is 14 years old, and the maximum age of contribution is 75 years old.

Employers are not allowed to calculate the employer's and employee's share based on the exact percentage, except for salaries that exceed RM20,000.00. The total contribution, including cents, shall be rounded up to the next ringgit.

These rates are effective for October 2025 salary/wage (the November 2025 contribution month).

## Monthly Contribution Rate (Third Schedule)

The latest contribution rate for employees and employers, effective October 2025 salary/wage, is set out in the Third Schedule of the EPF Act 1991, and employers are required to remit EPF contributions based on this schedule.

For late contribution payments, employers must remit contributions in accordance with the Third Schedule that applied on the relevant effective date. The Third Schedule rate tables published by EPF cover the following effective periods:

- Effective 1 October 2025 (October 2025 salary/wage onwards)
- Effective 1 July 2022 to 30 September 2025 (July 2022 salary/wage up to September 2025)
- Effective 1 January 2021 to 30 June 2022 (January 2021 salary/wage up to June 2022)
- Effective 1 April 2020 to 31 December 2020 (April 2020 salary/wage up to December 2020)
- Effective 1 January 2019 to 31 March 2020 (January 2019 salary/wage up to March 2020)
- Effective 1 January 2018 to 31 December 2018 (January 2018 salary/wage up to December 2018)
- Effective 1 March 2016 to 31 December 2017 (March 2016 salary/wage up to December 2017)
- Effective 1 August 2013 to 29 February 2016 (August 2013 salary/wage up to February 2016)
- Effective 1 January 2012 to 31 July 2013 (January 2012 salary/wage up to July 2013)
- Effective 1 January 2011 to 31 December 2011 (January 2011 salary/wage up to December 2011)

## Overdue Contributions

If the employer fails to pay the contributions within the prescribed period, the contribution is considered an outstanding contribution. Under certain circumstances, EPF will make an assessment of the contribution.

The EPF Officer will provide Form KWSP 7 (Form E) and Form KWSP 8 (Form F). Payment must then be made using Form KWSP 8 (Form F).

For unpaid outstanding contributions, payments can be made using Form A (Online).

## Late Payment Charge and Dividend

The Late Payment Charge dividend rate is the lower dividend rate between Simpanan Konvensional and Simpanan Shariah for each respective year, plus an additional one (1) percent.

The minimum late payment charge imposed is RM10. The late payment charge is rounded up to the nearest ringgit denomination. For example, a late payment charge of RM13.21 must be rounded up to RM14.

The dividend rate is calculated based on the lowest dividend declared between Simpanan Konvensional and Simpanan Shariah.

For the year 2016 and prior, Simpanan Konvensional's dividend rate is used for the calculation of the late payment charge and dividends.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is the definition of 'Employer'?** An employer is defined as a person or persons with whom an employee has a contract of service or apprenticeship. Employers include managers, agents or persons responsible for the payment of salary or wages to an employee; any group of people whether statutory or non-statutory or incorporated; and any government, government departments, statutory bodies, local authorities or other bodies specified in the Second Schedule, EPF Act 1991.

**What is the definition of 'Employee'?** An employee is defined as a person who is employed under a contract of service or apprenticeship. The contract of service or apprenticeship can be written or verbal, expressed or implied.

**What is the minimum age to register as an EPF member?** The minimum age to register as an EPF member is 14 years old.

**What does 'Late Payment Charge and Dividend' on late payment of contributions mean?** The Late Payment Charge is a fine imposed on employers in accordance with Section 49(1) of the EPF Act 1991 for failing to pay the monthly EPF contributions within the specified period stipulated by EPF. Apart from the Late Payment Charge, employers are also required to pay dividends accrued on late contributions received, for each month, at the current dividend rate set by the EPF Board for the year.

**What is 'Contribution'?** Contributions are a mandatory salary deduction comprising the employee's and employer's share that must be contributed within a specified period. The contribution for any given month is a deduction from the previous month's salary. The employer pays contributions based on the rates specified in the Third Schedule, EPF Act 1991 (subject to change). For example, the January 2014 salary deduction for the employee's and employer's shares is paid as the February contribution on or before 15 February 2014.

**What is a 'Domestic servant'?** According to Section 3 of the Workmen's Compensation Act 1952, domestic servants are individuals who work as, among others, maid, cook, gardener, cleaner, babysitter, driver and others.

**What is the 'Contribution Month'?** The contribution month is the month in which the contribution is payable based on the previous month's salary. For example, contributions paid in February 2014 are for January 2014's salary and must be paid on or before 15 February 2014.

**What are the 'Components of Wage'?** Payments that are subject to EPF contribution include salary/wages, bonus, allowance, commission, incentives, arrears of salaries/wages, payment in respect of unutilised annual or medical leave, paid maternity leave/wages for maternity leave, paid study leave/wages for study leave, wages for half-day leave, and other payments under a services contract or otherwise.

**What is the definition of 'Wages'?** Wages are all remuneration in the form of money paid by an employer to an employee under a contract of service or apprenticeship, based on agreed terms of payment, whether monthly, weekly or daily.

**What is the difference between a 'Contract of Service' and a 'Contract for Service'?** A Contract of Service is an agreement, mutually agreed between an employer and an employee, to complete a certain service; it may be written, verbal, expressed or implied. A Contract for Service is an agreement to provide service to a person or group of people, where the person providing the service is free from any control and monitoring by the person or group of people receiving the service.

**What are 'Non-Wages'?** Payments that are not liable for EPF contribution include service charge, overtime payment, gratuity, retirement benefits, retrenchment, temporary lay-off and termination benefits, payment in lieu of notice of termination of employment, and travelling allowance or the value of any travelling concession. This list is not exhaustive.

**Who are EPF Inspectors?** EPF Inspectors are officers appointed under Section 34 of the EPF Act 1991, empowered to carry out inspections on employers.

**Can the monthly contributions be paid bimonthly or combined as one month?** No. According to Section 43(1) of the EPF Act 1991, employers must pay on a monthly basis.

**What should an employer do to contribute more than the stipulated rate for the employer's share?** No form is required if employers opt to contribute more than the statutory rate for employers. Employers need to submit i-Topup (Employee's Share) and i-Topup (Employer's Share) applications through i-Akaun (Employer).

**If an employee chooses to contribute more than the stipulated rate, can the employer deduct it from the employee's own salary?** Yes, if the employee has opted for i-Topup (formerly known as Voluntary Excess) by submitting the application via the KWSP i-Akaun app or i-Akaun (Member) web portal, or by completing the KWSP 17A/18A (AHL) form (Permohonan Pendaftaran / Pembatalan i-Topup (Pilihan Mencarum Melebihi Kadar Berkanun (Syer Pekerja))).

**What is the calculation for the employer's and employee's shares for wages exceeding RM20,000.00?** For wages exceeding RM20,000.00 in a particular month, the employee's contribution rate is 11% of the total wages for the month and the employer's contribution rate is 12% of the total wages for the month. Total contributions, including cents, are rounded up to the next ringgit. For more information, refer to the Third Schedule, EPF Act 1991.

**Are arrears from an annual salary increment subject to EPF contribution?** Yes, and they are to be paid as contribution in the actual month. The annual salary increment is the fee payable for the actual month of the salary increment, so the contribution is paid as contribution for the actual month.

**Are salaries paid by the hour subject to EPF contribution?** Yes. If an employee works any day in a month and receives wages in excess of RM10.00 (whether daily, hourly or piece-rated salary), the employee is liable to make an EPF contribution.

**Is overtime payment subject to EPF contribution?** Overtime payments are not subject to EPF contribution.

**Are salary adjustments subject to EPF contribution?** Yes, and they are paid as the current contribution. A salary adjustment is not a scheduled payment and is not subject to any particular month, so its contribution is paid as contribution for the current month.

**Are wages paid for work during holidays (public holidays, annual leave) subject to EPF contribution?** Yes, unless the work attendance is considered as overtime.

**Who does 'Employee Liable to Contribute' refer to?** It refers to permanent employees; contract employees; part-time employees; temporary employees; probationary employees; directors receiving salary/wages; employees who have made a full withdrawal under the Incapacitation Withdrawal and have since recovered and are re-employed in any service; employees who have made a 55 Years Withdrawal and are below 75 years old and still working in any occupation; employees who have made a Pensionable Employees and Optional Retirement Withdrawal and are working with employers other than the Federal or State Governments or any statutory bodies or local authorities; employees who have made a Pensionable Employees Withdrawal and were re-employed after the retirement age; and non-Malaysian citizen employees with a valid passport and work pass (excluding domestic workers).

**Is a driver's trip allowance subject to EPF contribution?** Yes. Trip allowance is included in wage elements and is therefore subject to contribution.

**Is a part-time worker or an employee who works with more than one employer subject to EPF contribution?** Yes, they are subject to EPF contribution.

**When is an employee eligible for EPF contribution?** An employee is eligible to contribute upon receiving a salary of more than RM10.00 in a month or part thereof. For example, an employee who worked for one day in March 2013 and received a wage of RM10.01 is eligible.

**What is the minimum number of employees before contribution is required?** One person.

**Do foreign companies hiring Malaysian citizens need to contribute?** Yes, if the foreign company has registered and is operating in Malaysia.

**Are directors required to register as a member and contribute to EPF?** All salaried directors are required to register as a member and contribute to EPF.

**Is there a liability to contribute for deceased employees?** Employers are not liable to make any contribution for the month an employee passes away (Section 43(7), EPF Act 1991).

**In what situation will employers be charged with the Late Payment Charge and Dividend?** The Late Payment Charge and Dividend are imposed for late contribution payments. The Late Payment Charge is imposed when payment for a particular month is made later than the 15th of the month. The dividend is imposed when the payment for the contribution month is made from the 1st of the following month. Late payment of contributions includes overdue contributions and underpaid contributions. For example, if an employer pays arrears for February 2014 on 20 February 2014, they are charged a Late Payment Charge only; if an employer pays arrears for February 2014 on 2 March 2014, they are charged both the Late Payment Charge and the Dividend.

**If the 15th falls on a public holiday and payment is made on the next working day, will the employer still be imposed with a Late Payment Charge?** No. The deadline for the monthly contribution payment is the 15th of each month, but if the 15th falls on a public holiday or weekend, payment made on the next working day is not imposed with a Late Payment Charge.

**Can an employer deduct the employer's share of contribution from an employee's salary?** No. According to Section 47(1) of the EPF Act 1991, any employer who makes or attempts to make deductions from an employee's wages or remuneration, in whole or in any part, of the employer's contribution faces imprisonment of no more than six years, or a fine of not more than RM20,000, or both.

**Can an employer deduct the employee's share of contribution from an employee's wage?** Yes, employers may deduct the employee's share from wages paid for that particular month only. According to Section 48(3) of the EPF Act 1991, any employer who deducts contributions from an employee's wage but fails to pay the deducted sum or any part of it to EPF faces imprisonment of no more than six years, or a fine of not more than RM20,000, or both.

**What are the employer's responsibilities?** Register as an EPF employer within 7 days from the date of hiring workers; register employees as EPF members; keep books and sign wages/salaries; provide salary statements/payslips; pay EPF contributions; and inform EPF of contrasting information between the EPF statement and employer records, any change of address and name/status of the employer, termination of the liability to contribute, and being temporarily not liable to contribute (no employees).

**What are the Director's responsibilities?** Directors share the same responsibilities as employers.

**What is the scope of authority for EPF Inspectors?** The inspectors' scope of authority is as stated in Section 35 of the EPF Act 1991, and includes entering any premise or location believed to have hired individuals as employees; carrying out inspections and probes as and when necessary to ensure the provisions of the Act, or rules and regulations under it, are being observed and complied with; and cross-examining, either alone or in the presence of any other person, on matters pertaining to the Act as he may reasonably require.
