# EIS (Employment Insurance System / SIP) — Employer and Employee Contributions

Source: PERKESO — Contributions
— from PERKESO (Social Security Organisation), "Contributions", "Contribution Rate", and "Contribution Payment": https://www.perkeso.gov.my/en/our-services/employer-employee/contributions.html

The Employment Insurance System (EIS) is governed by the Employment Insurance System Act 2017 (Act 800), which came into force on 1 January 2018. EIS contributions are administered and collected by PERKESO.

## Coverage

All employers in the private sector are required to pay monthly contributions on behalf of each employee. Government employees, domestic workers, and the self-employed are exempt.

An employee is defined as a person who is employed for wages under a contract of service or apprenticeship with an employer. The contract of service or apprenticeship may be expressed or implied and may be oral or in writing.

All employees aged 18 to 60 are required to contribute. However, employees aged 57 and above who have no prior contributions before the age of 57 are exempt.

## Contribution Rate

Contributions to the Employment Insurance System are set at 0.4% of the employee's assumed monthly salary. Of this, the employer pays 0.2% of the employee's assumed monthly salary and 0.2% is deducted from the employee's monthly salary.

The contribution rates are set out in the Second Schedule and are subject to the rules in Section 18 of the Employment Insurance System Act 2017. Employers in the private sector are required to pay monthly contributions on behalf of each employee, while government employees, domestic workers, and the self-employed are exempt.

## Wage Ceiling

Contribution rates are capped at an assumed monthly salary of RM6,000. For an employee earning more than RM6,000 per month, contributions are calculated as if the monthly salary were RM6,000.

Effective 1 October 2024, PERKESO raised the wage ceiling for contributions from RM5,000 to RM6,000 per month. For an employee whose salary exceeds RM5,000 per month, the contribution amount is stated in the Second Schedule of Act 800, and for an employee whose salary exceeds RM6,000 per month the contribution amount is subject to the wage ceiling of RM6,000. The contribution payment for October 2024 and subsequent months must be made according to this new wage ceiling.

## Contribution Payment Period

Because EIS is collected by PERKESO, contributions payable for any month must be paid no later than the 15th day of the succeeding month. For example, contributions for July 2017 must be paid no later than 15 August 2017.

## Interest on Late Payment of Contributions

Interest on late payment of contributions is imposed at a rate of 6% per annum for each day that contributions are not paid within the stipulated period.

## Eligibility to Claim Benefits

All employees insured under the Act (known as Insured Persons) who have lost their employment are eligible to claim benefits, with certain exceptions.

An Insured Person is not eligible where the loss of employment is due to voluntary resignation by the Insured Person.

An Insured Person is not eligible where the loss of employment is due to the expiry of the Insured Person's fixed-term contract.

An Insured Person is not eligible where the loss of employment is due to the unconditional termination of a contract of service based on an agreement between the Insured Person and the employer.

An Insured Person is not eligible where the loss of employment is due to the completion of a project specified in a contract of service.

An Insured Person is not eligible where the loss of employment is due to the retirement of the Insured Person.

An Insured Person is not eligible where the loss of employment is due to dismissal for misconduct by the Insured Person.

Applicants for benefits must prove that they are able to work, available to work, and actively seeking work.
