# SOCSO (PERKESO) — Employees' Social Security Contributions

Source: PERKESO — Contributions
— from PERKESO (Social Security Organisation), "Contributions", "Contribution Rate", and "Contribution Payment": https://www.perkeso.gov.my/en/our-services/employer-employee/contributions.html

SOCSO is administered under the Employees' Social Security Act 1969 (Act 4). Employers are required to pay monthly contributions for each eligible employee according to the rate specified under the Act. These contributions are divided into two types: the First Category and the Second Category.

## First Category (Employees Below 60 Years of Age)

For an employee who is less than 60 years of age, contributions payable by the employer and the employee cover both the Employment Injury Scheme and the Invalidity Scheme. Under this First Category, the employer contributes 1.75% of the employee's monthly wages and the employee contributes 0.5% of the employee's monthly wages, according to the contribution schedule.

All employees who have not reached the age of 60 must contribute under the First Category, except for those who have attained 55 years of age and have no prior contributions before they reached 55 due to non-eligibility under the Employees' Social Security Act 1969.

## Second Category (Employees Aged 60 and Above)

For an employee who has reached the age of 60, contributions cover the Employment Injury Scheme only. Under this Second Category, the employer contributes 1.25% of the employee's monthly wages and the employee contributes nothing, according to the contribution schedule.

All employees who have reached the age of 60 must be covered under the Second Category.

For an eligible new employee who is 55 years of age, the employee must be covered under the Second Category.

## Wage Ceiling

Effective 1 October 2024, PERKESO enforces a wage ceiling for contributions of RM6,000 per month, raised from the previous ceiling of RM5,000 per month.

For an employee whose salary exceeds RM5,000 per month, the contribution amount is stated in the Third Schedule of Act 4.

For an employee whose salary exceeds RM6,000 per month, the contribution amount is subject to the wage ceiling of RM6,000, so contributions are calculated as if the monthly wage were RM6,000.

The contribution payment for October 2024 and subsequent months must be made by the employer according to this new wage ceiling.

## Foreign Workers and Domestic Workers

The contribution rates for Foreign Workers refer to the Third Schedule of Act 4.

## Contribution Payment Period

Contributions payable for any month must be paid no later than the 15th day of the succeeding month. For example, contributions for July 2017 must be paid no later than 15 August 2017.

## Interest on Late Payment of Contributions

Interest on late payment of contributions is imposed at a rate of 6% per annum for each day that contributions are not paid within the stipulated period.

## Payment Channels

Since 1 January 2018, PERKESO has provided the PERKESO ASSIST Portal to help employers manage their registration, update records, and make contribution payments. To use the portal, employers must register to obtain an ASSIST Portal ID by submitting the relevant form to the nearest PERKESO office.

Employers can pay contributions through Financial Process Exchange (FPX), which requires an internet banking account with a bank offering FPX services.

Employers can pay contributions through Direct Debit Authorisation (DDA), which requires an application for an e-mandate through the ASSIST Portal.

Employers can pay contributions through internet banking with appointed banks where they hold an account.

Employers can pay contributions at bank counters using an ACR reference with contribution data submitted through the PERKESO ASSIST Portal. The banks appointed by PERKESO as collection agents are Maybank Berhad and Public Bank Berhad (the latter starting 1 March 2018).

Employers can also download the PRIHATIN mobile app from the Google Play Store or App Store for more information and services.
